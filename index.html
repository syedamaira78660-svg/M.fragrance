Our Story

Built by One Woman, One Bottle at a Time

M. Fragrance started with a simple belief: smelling incredible shouldn't come with a designer price tag. As a young entrepreneur launching her very first product, Momal spent months testing and refining four signature scents before she was happy enough to put her name on them.

This perfume collection is just the beginning — candles and more are already in the works. For now, every bottle is packed, checked, and shipped by hand.

"I wanted everyone to feel confident in their own scent — without paying designer prices."

— Momal, Founder
